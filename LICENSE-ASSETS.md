## What this covers

- **Panel designs** — the front panel artwork and layout for every ForgeSeries
  module, in every form: the sources under [PanelDesign/](PanelDesign/), the
  exported panel images under `apps/*/images/`, the shared panel kit under
  [vcv/design/](vcv/design/), and the `.svg` panels shipped in the VCV Rack
  plugin under `apps/*/vcv-plugin/res/` and [vcv/res/](vcv/res/).
- **Logos and brand marks** — `VFM-Logo-Full.png` and every other Voltage
  Foundry Modular logo or mark.
- **Names** — "Voltage Foundry Modular", "ForgeSeries", "VFM", and the module
  names ClockForge, NoteForge, GravityForge, ForgeView, ChaosForge and
  WeaveForge, together with the vfmod.com identity.

These are not licensed under the GPL and are not placed in the public domain.

## What you may do

The point of this file is that the GPL grant on the code stays genuinely
usable — a licence that let you have the source but not build or run it would be
worth nothing. So:

1. **Build, run, modify and study the software**, panels included. You may
   compile the firmware and the VCV Rack plugin with the artwork in place, flash
   it to hardware you own, and run it.

2. **Distribute your modified version** of the software with the artwork intact,
   as the GPL requires — provided you do **not** present it as an official
   Voltage Foundry Modular release, and you make clear it is a modified,
   unofficial build.

3. **Refer to the modules by name** factually — reviews, tutorials, patch notes,
   videos, compatibility statements, "works with ForgeSeries". Nominative use
   needs no permission.

## What you may not do

1. **Manufacture or sell hardware** bearing these panel designs, logos or module
   names, or anything confusingly similar.

2. **Use the names or marks** for your own product, brand, plugin or repository
   in a way that suggests it is ours, endorsed by us, or affiliated with us.

3. **Reuse the artwork** — panels, logos, icons — in unrelated projects or
   products, or sell it.

4. **Publish a plugin to the VCV Library** using these panels or module names.

## Why it is split this way

The code is GPL because the ideas in it should be free to learn from, fork and
build on. The brand is not, because it is the only thing separating a module I
stand behind from one I have never seen. Fork the code, put your own face on it.

## Permission

These restrictions are about identity, not about being difficult. If you want to
do something the above does not allow — sell hardware, build a variant that
keeps the look, use a panel somewhere — ask. Contact details are at
[vfmod.com](https://vfmod.com/).
